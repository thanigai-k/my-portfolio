### I’m a Senior Frontend Engineer who cares deeply about how systems feel — not just to users, but to the engineers who build on top of them every day. Over time, my work has naturally shifted toward solving the messy, recurring problems that slow teams down: inconsistent UI patterns, fragile components, unclear interaction models and design systems that exist in name but not in practice.

When I join a team, I focus on bringing clarity and structure to the frontend ecosystem. I help teams move from one-off implementations to thoughtful patterns, from UI chaos to shared foundations, and from reactive fixes to intentional engineering decisions. My impact tends to show up in fewer regressions, faster product development, more confident engineers, and a frontend platform that feels stable rather than fragile.

I enjoy working on the hard parts — accessibility that actually works in production, complex interaction behaviour, component APIs that don’t fight developers, and architectural decisions that stand the test of time. I’m most energised when I can improve how an entire organisation builds UI, not just ship isolated features. You can explore examples of my work in [projects](/projects)
and read my [thoughts](/thoughts) on design, engineering, and frontend systems in blog / notes
.

Right now, I’m looking for a team where frontend engineering is taken seriously — where design systems are real infrastructure, product quality matters, and engineers are trusted to shape how things are built. I’m excited by opportunities where I can bring structure, raise the bar for frontend practices, and leave the system stronger than I found it.
